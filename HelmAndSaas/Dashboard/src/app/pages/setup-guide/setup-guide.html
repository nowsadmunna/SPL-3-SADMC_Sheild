  • helm (v3+) installed locally
  • Network reachability from the cluster to the SaaS endpoint below

Step 4 — Verify

  • kubectl logs -n sadmc -l app=sadmc-agent -f should show a discovery + inference cycle every 15 seconds.
  • Your cluster will appear in the selector at the top of this dashboard within a few seconds.
  • Check the Overview page for discovered services, or the Anomaly Feed for live detections.
